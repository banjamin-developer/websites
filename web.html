Dr. Aparna Bhasin

Director of Clinical Radiology

Dr. Aparna Bhasin is a Senior Consultant Radiologist with over 15 years of experience spanning clinical radiology and medical AI. An alumna of Maulana Azad Medical College, University of Delhi, where she completed her medical education with honours, she brings deep clinical expertise in diagnostic imaging, anatomy, pathology, and the role of medical imaging in patient care.

Over the past decade, she has combined her clinical knowledge with the technical demands of AI development, working extensively in medical image annotation, segmentation, expert review, and validation. Her expertise lies in bridging clinical interpretation and AI training data, ensuring that datasets are not only technically accurate but also clinically meaningful and relevant to real-world diagnostic scenarios. At Cogito Tech, she brings clinical expertise to the development of high-quality medical AI training datasets. She also leads and coordinates multidisciplinary teams of radiologists, medical specialists, and annotators, overseeing complex segmentation workflows and establishing review processes that promote accuracy, consistency, and quality across datasets.

Raj Verma

Head - Human Resources

Raj Verma is Human Resources Head in Cogito. In this role, Raj leads Cogito’s Human Resources (HR) function and plays an integral role in the company’s ongoing journey to be an employer of choice.

He joined Cogito in 2017 and led HR engagements across various businesses. He was appointed as HR Manager in 2017 and has since been part of the leadership team. He began his career with Unique Personnel (U.K.) Limited as HR Support Executive in United Kingdom. Raj has MBA degree from University of Wales and diploma in project management from Heriot-Watt University, UK.
